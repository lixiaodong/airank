What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?

GEO is the practice of making a business correctly identified, accurately described and recommended inside answers generated by AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity. SEO competes for a position on a results page; GEO competes for the sentence inside the answer itself. It is also called AEO (Answer Engine Optimization).

Can you submit my business to ChatGPT so it gets indexed?

No, and nobody can. Large language models have no submission or indexing mechanism. What can actually be delivered is making your facts easy for AI to retrieve, verify and quote when it searches the web, and placing those facts on sources the AI actually checks.

How do you prove it worked?

The same fixed set of prompts is run before and after the work. You receive a before/after comparison of mention rate, factual accuracy and the number of platforms that identify you correctly, with screenshots, plus the full prompt list so you can reproduce every result yourself.
